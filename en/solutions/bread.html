FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does bread go stale quickly in the machine?

Not at room temperature with daily rotation — cooling actually makes bread go stale faster.

How often does the machine need restocking?

Usually once or twice a day, matched to morning and evening peaks.

Can it sell rolls and loaves at the same time?

Yes, we configure the feeders for different package sizes.
